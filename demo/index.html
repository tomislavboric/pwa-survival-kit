PWA Survival Kit

Three small browser utilities for PWA problems that only show up in production: chunk 404s after a deploy, the iOS home-screen short viewport, and multi-megabyte camera photos.

01

On-device photo compression

compressImage(file, options) downscales to a maximum long edge and lowers JPEG quality until the photo fits a byte target — before anything goes over a weak mobile connection. Nothing leaves your device on this page.

02

Deploy-skew recovery

After a new deploy, a tab still running the old build asks for chunk files that no longer exist, and the lazy import() fails. The first failure reloads once with a marker; a second failure within the cooldown means the reload did not help, so instead of looping it shows a refresh screen.

  1. Click once: a real import() of a missing chunk fails → recovery asks for a reload (simulated here, the page stays).
  2. Click again within 15 s: the "reloaded" page fails the same way → the refresh screen appears instead of another reload.
  3. Wait 15 s: an unrelated later failure gets its own free reload again.

    03

    iOS standalone viewport fix

    iOS home-screen PWAs sometimes launch with a browser-sized layout viewport: innerHeight, 100dvh and env(safe-area-inset-bottom) come up short by the Safari chrome band, and nothing recomputes until the first scroll. A full-screen splash stops above the bottom edge and a fixed bottom nav floats mid-screen. armStandaloneViewportFix() detects the shortfall and forces the recompute with an invisible scroll nudge and reflow, with bounded retries.

    To see it for real: open this page in Safari on an iPhone, Share → Add to Home Screen, and launch it from the icon. This page arms the fix on load; attempts are listed below when it runs.
